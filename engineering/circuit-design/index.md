# Circuit Design

These pages hold the circuit-design rules that every Scadys board follows. Each product's own Circuit Design section explains its circuits sheet by sheet, and refers back to these pages where a rule applies.

| Section | Covers |
|---|---|
| [Principles](./principles/index.md) | How a board is laid out so that a prototype can be assembled, probed, isolated, programmed and repaired by hand. |
| [Parts](./parts/index.md) | Rules for particular part families, where the part's footprint or placement decides whether it survives assembly and service. |
