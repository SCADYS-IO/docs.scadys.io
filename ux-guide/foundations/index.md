# Foundations

The Foundations are the rules every page follows before it shows any particular data. They set how a page is read, how it is coloured and lit, where things may sit and how it answers a touch.

| Page | Covers |
|---|---|
| [Legibility](./legibility.md) | Reading distance and conditions, the brightest element on a page, and why legibility wins over effects |
| [Colour](./colour.md) | Colour roles, the day and night palettes, and the colours reserved for one meaning |
| [Typography](./typography.md) | The two typefaces, the type roles and their sizes, and digits that hold still |
| [Layout](./layout.md) | The 720 × 720 canvas, the whole-pixel grid, the corner safe area and the standard data-page layouts |
| [Materials and depth](./materials-and-depth.md) | Flat surfaces, the one recess treatment, shadows by day and glows at night, and needles behind readouts |
| [Motion](./motion.md) | Changing digits, the alert throb, needle smoothing and the timing of feedback |
| [Night](./night.md) | Dark adaptation, the amber night palette and the backlight as the night dimmer |
| [Touch accessibility](./touch-accessibility.md) | One finger, action on release, target sizes for gloved hands, and water on the glass |

## Display-neutral by default

The Foundations describe the design rather than one panel. A rule holds for every SCADYS display and for the planned mobile app unless it names a product. Pixel values are on the 720 × 720 design canvas described under [Layout](./layout.md).
